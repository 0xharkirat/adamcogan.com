I’m thrilled to share that we’ve just launched **EasyLeave** — a brand new feature in **[SSW TimePro](https://sswtimepro.com/)** that makes managing leave a breeze.

People don’t want many steps to take their leave. In a perfect world, with a few clicks it’s done and in the right people’s calendars and their timesheets are also done.

With EasyLeave, welcome to the perfect world. It’s simple, fast, and integrated with time tracking.

From the management side, there is full visibility, better reporting, and fewer scheduling headaches.

We built EasyLeave using a [modular monolith](https://www.ssw.com.au/rules/rules-to-better-modular-monoliths) approach on .NET 8 (now .NET 9), which means tighter integration, faster delivery, and a smoother developer experience (DX).

If you’re already using TimePro, you’ll see the new ****EasyLeave**** tab is live and ready to use.

Learn more on [SSW TimePro blog – 🎉 Introducing EasyLeave – Smarter Leave Management in SSW TimePro](https://sswtimepro.com/introducing-easyleave/).

Thanks for being part of the journey, and enjoy your next day off (with a little help from EasyLeave)!
