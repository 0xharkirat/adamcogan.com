September has come and gone and NDC Sydney is over. SSW was a large part of this great conference, which is personally my favourite event of the year.

Of course, I love going to NDC Oslo, but there’s nothing like having the Norwegians come to your hometown. This was the 3rd annual Sydney event and it was awesome. The conference is a 5-day event (September 18-21), composed of 2 days of workshops and 3 days of presentations. SSW and lots of companies really work hard for this event – we made sure our guys were presenting leading-edge technologies and practices and we also had a cool booth and cool marketing collateral, such as t-shirts and brochures, a live Azure conversation bot, a HoloLens and more.

This year, a bunch of SSW developers worked really hard to have a conversation bot working against live data that has real business benefits. It used Azure, Google, Dynamics CRM, Office 365, SharePoint, and answered some really cool questions. Our team worked really hard and finished it the night before the event!

SSW was an event partner, so we had a booth showing off our Sophie-bot and our Sophie-receptionist, and everyone looked great in our 3 new SSW t-shirts (see below!).

## SSW TV and .NET Rocks!

I’m proud to announce that this year we had our own track at the conference! We shared the top room with [Carl Franklin](https://twitter.com/carlfranklin) and [Richard Campbell](http://twitter.com/richcampbell) from the [.NET Rocks podcast](https://dotnetrocks.com/), and took turns recording Ask Me Anything! sessions and .NET Rocks podcasts. And of course, the [SSW TV](http://tv.ssw.com) team were there to film and live stream everything – so don’t worry if you missed our live stream. We’ll be releasing all of the videos on SSW TV as soon as they’re ready.
