What’s worse than moving offices and doing a reno?
Doing 2 at the same time – which is what happened with SSW Melbourne and SSW Brisbane!
The new spaces are so great but the effort was huge. I deserve a party! 🍺🎈🍺🎈

I’m delighted to announce that SSW is holding parties at SSW Melbourne and SSW Brisbane to celebrate the opening of our 2 new offices \*this month\*.

If you’re a fan of [SSW TV](https://tv.ssw.com/), or [SSW Rules](https://rules.ssw.com.au/), or if you’ve followed SSW for some time, let’s celebrate!

Join us for finger food, drinks and a chance to meet the team. I’ll also do a short talk, 7 Simple Steps to Building your Profile.

**Melbourne:**
6pm Thursday February 15 (the day after our 1st [Melbourne .NET User Group](https://www.meetup.com/Melbourne-NET-User-Group/events/244479054/), which I also recommend you get along to!)
Tickets: [http://r.ssw.com/Melbourne-Opening-Party](http://r.ssw.com/Melbourne-Opening-Party)

**Brisbane:**
6pm Friday February 16 is Brisbane’s turn!
Tickets: [http://r.ssw.com/Brisbane-Opening-Party](http://r.ssw.com/Brisbane-Opening-Party)
