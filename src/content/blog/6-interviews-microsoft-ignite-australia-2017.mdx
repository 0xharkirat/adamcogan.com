Microsoft Ignite Australia 2017 was a blast last week on the Gold Coast. [SSW](https://ssw.com.au) was well represented with our guys there and we had a good looking stand. I did 2 presentations that went well and will be posted soon. In addition, I did 6 interviews which are posted below. The [SSW TV](https://tv.ssw.com) team worked like demons and produced 26 videos in only 4 days. I will blog about that shortly.

#### Interview: Azure on a Coffee Budget with Troy Hunt

Troy Hunt speaks about his talk at Microsoft Ignite Australia on “Building a Large Scale Real World Application on a Coffee Budget”.

He explains why he likes New Relic over App Insights and how when developers write good code, it can save you money on your monthly Azure bill.

#### Interview: DevOps in the wild with Hamish Watson

In this interview, Hamish Watson covers what he believes are the important elements of good DevOps:

1.  How to truly automate apps and databases
2.  How to work towards a common goal of repeatable automated deployments using continuous integration and continuous delivery
3.  The pillars of tools, process, people and culture
4.  VSTS Release Manager vs Octopus Deploy
5.  DACPAC vs Entity Framework vs manual scripts

#### Interview: The 5 Languages of Appreciation in the Workplace with Kylie Hunt

Kylie discusses the 5 Love Languages and the impact they have on the workplace, and how managers can use these elements to understand what motivates their employees and how they can show their appreciation in the workplace.

Kylie also summarizes the 5 Languages of Appreciation which include:

1.  Words of affirmation
2.  Quality time
3.  Acts of service
4.  Tangible gifts
5.  Physical touch
