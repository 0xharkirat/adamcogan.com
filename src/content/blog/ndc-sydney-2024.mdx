There were some Microsoft heavy hitters in attendance including Scott Hanselman, Damien Edwards, and David Fowler, as well as international rockstars like Keynote speaker David Whitney and .NET Rocks presenter Richard Campbell and more. There were a lot of really friendly, smart people everywhere! It’s not just the talks that you benefit from when you attend a conference like NDC, but the side chats and conversations that happen all around that make a huge impact.

Scott Guthrie was also in Australia the week before for an AI conference, and I had a great time catching up with him too! We had a fascinating chat about everything from Nuclear Power to AI.

## Scott Hansleman’s Keynote

It was awesome to see Scott Hansleman again. Scott’s talk “Iron Man or Ultron: Is AI here to help us or hurt us?” was a fascinating insight into AI’s increasing influence and power, and whether or not it was going to be more harmful or good. He also drew from his extensive experience to look at proactive ways you could prevent AI from becoming a source of harm. Scott attended my session prior, so he was able to juxtapose my solutions vs how he would develop the same solution. I felt his talk was tailored to me. 😂 Now I have alternate ways to try to implement.

## My AI Talk

My presentation was called “ChatGPT and OpenAI API solutions: 👑 My Favourite Implementations”. For this talk, I was going to reuse what I did in Oslo, but in 6 months, I could only really use a few slides because AI is moving so fast. So, I decided to walk through a bunch of the AI solutions that we have developed for clients and ourselves. AI has touched every person’s job in SSW and putting together the slides gave me a number of ‘oh wow’ moments.
